SECTION 01

Origin & The Macro Thesis

In traditional economic frameworks, ownership of high-value physical assets—ranging from specialized construction equipment, enterprise hardware, and industrial cameras to consumer vehicles and creator gear—is treated as a binary state: you either purchase with significant upfront capital, or you undergo convoluted, long-term leasing agreements.

The result is massive capital stagnation: trillions of dollars in physical utility sits dormant over 80% of its operating lifetime. Mayank Yadav founded MRIG to transform static physical ownership into liquid, yield-generating computational assets through dynamic market matching, automated trust verification, and modular escrow protocols.

Core Thesis: "Every physical asset with recurring utility should possess a liquid, transparent, and frictionless transactional lifecycle—unlocking yield for owners and flexible on-demand access for operators."

SECTION 02

The Multi-Trillion Dollar Problem Space

The physical asset marketplace landscape is plagued by structural market inefficiencies that hinder velocity and trust:

  • Severe Asset Illiquidity: Owners lack standardized secondary channels to rent or fractionalize equipment without substantial administrative overhead.
  • Counterparty Trust & Escrow Friction: Peer-to-peer and B2B asset custody transfers suffer from high fraud rates, security deposit disputes, and manual verification delays.
  • Fragmented Asset Financing & Micro-Insurance: Traditional insurers and underwriters do not offer dynamic, per-use or micro-duration coverage for transient asset rentals.
  • Talent Matching Disconnect: On the workforce side, organizations acquiring equipment struggle equally with fragmented candidate qualification, unverified resumes, and protracted hiring cycles.

SECTION 04

Marketplace Model

The marketplace operates on a multi-sided network structure:

  • Asset Suppliers & Fleet Owners: List idle inventory, configure dynamic availability windows, receive collateralized payments, and track equipment custody seamlessly.
  • Asset Renters & Operators: Access top-tier physical equipment on demand with zero long-term capex debt, instant micro-insurance coverage, and automated escrow protection.
  • Financial & Insurance Partners: Underwrite verified asset batches and provide instant credit lines based on verifiable utilization metrics.

SECTION 06

Technology Architecture & Systems

MRIG is built upon modern distributed infrastructure, dynamic pricing algorithms, and automated escrow settlement:

  • Dynamic Asset Valuation Model (AVM): Real-time pricing algorithms that calculate optimal rental rates based on depreciation curves, demand density, seasonality, and geographical radius.
  • Programmable Escrow Protocols: Automated deposit holding and collateral distribution ensuring zero-risk custody transfer between unknown counterparties.
  • AI Candidate Vector Embeddings: Semantic matching engine that indexes candidate experience against employer technical requirements with high precision.
SECTION 07

Business Model & Monetization Architecture

MRIG's unit economics and monetization strategy are architected around transparent, transaction-aligned revenue channels:

  • Marketplace Commission (12% – 18%): Transaction take rate on completed peer-to-peer and commercial equipment rentals.
  • Escrow & Instant Settlement Processing: Value-added margin for collateralized escrow guarantees and rapid supplier payouts.
  • Embedded Micro-Insurance Brokerage: Revenue share on dynamic per-use damage and theft protection underwritten with institutional partners.
  • Enterprise GetNextIn Screening Tier: B2B subscription pricing for automated high-volume candidate screening and verified talent pipelines.
