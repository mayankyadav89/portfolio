The Founder's Journey

My trajectory began with rigorous training in Computer Science & Engineering with a formal specialization in Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning at UIT RGPV. Fascinated by distributed state machines and neural architectures, I quickly realized that the true potential of software lies not in isolated academic algorithms, but in building operational systems that eliminate friction in physical human economies.

As Founder & CEO of MRIG, I am currently directing the parent company infrastructure powering Rentro (physical asset marketplace, rentro.mrig.tech) to unlock the latent value of underutilized fleets and commercial machinery, and GetNextIn (getnextin.mrig.tech) to re-engineer technical candidate screening and verified talent pipelines.

Previously, I founded Crypticard to tackle decentralized identity and verifiable credentials, led India Business Development for Koii Network scaling active developer communities past 2,500+ members, and researched protocol token economics at Victus Global.

Mental Models & Architecture

Founder Operating Principles

The four non-negotiable mental models that guide how I architect systems, build ventures, and lead ecosystems.

PRINCIPLE 01

Systems Over Gimmicks

True technological value is created by solving tangible economic friction points, not by chasing speculative cycles. Every line of code and business model must deliver durable utility.

PRINCIPLE 02

Frontier Integration

The most profound breakthroughs happen at the intersection of disciplines: marrying AI prediction models with decentralized cryptographic settlement to eliminate middlemen.

PRINCIPLE 03

Execution Rigor

A vision without relentless customer discovery and continuous prototyping is merely hallucination. We validate hypotheses directly with operators, renters, and developers.

PRINCIPLE 04

Community as a Moat

Durable technology platforms are sustained by empowered, aligned developer and builder ecosystems. Authentic technical leadership builds lasting network effects.
